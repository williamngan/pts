# 1.0.1 release review

Review of everything since the `v1.0.0` tag (2026-09-19): Poisson-disk
sampling, the curve-to-Bezier conversions with `form.bezier`, and the `Path`
boolean operations with `form.compound`. Three independent read-throughs
(Path; sampling and curves; documentation) plus my own reproduction of every
finding marked confirmed. Scratch probes live in the session scratchpad and
are not part of the repo.

Gate on entry: typecheck, lint and 553 node tests pass; `check:docs` fails on
stale `guide.md` (see Docs). An A/B bench against the v1.0.0 build shows no
regression in shared code (Delaunay and the scenarios suite unchanged, deltas
inside noise). The `--against` A/B cannot run the `op` or `create` suites
because the old build lacks `Path`, `PoissonDisk` and the conversions, and the
runner crashes on the first missing function instead of skipping the case.

## Must fix before publishing

1. **Path: memory and time grow quadratically with overlap depth**
   (`src/_path.ts` `label`). Each face copies the full sparse winding map of
   its neighbor, so faces × overlap depth maps are held at once. Confirmed:
   uniting thin bars through one center costs 67 MB at 100 bars, 395 MB at
   200, 1.27 GB at 300, and 500 bars need 5 GB; 1000 bars kills the process.
   400 overlapping 64-gon discs on a 400 px canvas, an ordinary sketch, take
   1.5 s and 1.2 GB. Fix: `select` only needs the map's size and whether
   shapes 0 and k−1 are present, so label by a DFS over the dual graph that
   keeps one mutable winding vector (apply the edge deltas on descent, undo on
   return) and stores three scalars per face.

2. **Path: the candidate-pair list overflows on a fan of shapes sharing a
   vertex** (`src/_path.ts` `sweep`). Pairs are materialized in an array, so a
   fan of 6000 triangles sharing the origin throws `RangeError: Invalid array
length`; 4000 take 1.1 s and 677 MB. Fix: stream pairs to a callback and
   skip pairs that share a vertex before the touch test; a y-bucketed sweep
   would remove the quadratic candidate count altogether (2000 full-width
   disjoint bars already spend 32 M y-tests).

3. **`Curve.cardinalToBezier` returns NaN points for a non-finite or huge
   `alpha`** (`src/Op.ts` guard at the top of the function). The guard rejects
   negative and NaN alpha but `Math.pow(distance, alpha)` overflows to Infinity
   for `alpha = Infinity` or `alpha = 400` at pixel scale, and the weights
   become `0 · (∞/∞)`. Confirmed. Fix: guard `!(alpha >= 0) ||
!Number.isFinite(alpha)`, and treat a non-finite interval like a zero one
   (constant segment). Add a test for both.

4. **Version and changelog.** `package.json` is still 1.0.0; `docs.md` and
   `guide.md` embed the version, so regenerate docs after the bump.
   `CHANGELOG.md` needs a `## 1.0.1 (2026-09-19)` heading and the 1.0.0
   subsection structure (`### New`, `### Fixed`, `### Internal / tooling`).
   Specific gaps: the `PoissonDisk` bullet's continuation line lost its list
   indent and breaks the item; the second Path bullet is implementation detail
   and belongs under internal; `PolygonLike` and `PoissonDiskOptions` types,
   the `PoissonDisk` getters (`radius`, `candidates`, `bound`, `done`) and
   what `setup` throws on are not named; the demo removal (`htmlform.scope`)
   and rename (`svgform.scope` → `svgspace.getForm`) are unrecorded.

5. **Stale generated docs and editor list.** `check:docs` fails because
   `guide.md` still carries the previous `path.crop` description. The editor
   sidebar (`demo/edit/index.html`) lists neither `create.sampling` nor
   `path.crop`. Order for regeneration: bump version, `pnpm build`, `pnpm run
docs`, `node scripts/build-editor.mjs`.

## Should fix, small

6. **Path: a single Group passed instead of a list throws `TypeError: it is
not iterable`** (`src/_path.ts` `ringsOf`). `Path.unite(square)` is the
   likeliest misuse since every `Polygon.*` function takes one Group. Detect
   `typeof list[0][0] === "number"` and either wrap it as one shape or warn.
   Related: `form.compound(Path.divide(...))` (a `Group[][]`) draws nothing
   silently because the first "point" is a Pt; a `typeof p[0][0] !==
"number"` warning would catch it.

7. **Path: pinched rings are undocumented.** A hole touching its outer ring,
   or two holes touching at a vertex, come back as one ring with a repeated
   non-consecutive vertex. Confirmed: `Path.minusFront([sq(0,0,10),
[[0,5],[5,8],[5,2]]])` returns one ring `0,0 10,0 10,10 0,10 0,5 5,8 5,2
0,5`. Nonzero fill draws it correctly and `divide` gives the same face as
   two rings, so the modes disagree on shape. Either split cycles at repeated
   vertices into outer plus holes, or say in the `Path` class doc that rings
   may touch themselves at a vertex. Pin whichever with a test.

8. **Path: a shape that collapses entirely under the tolerance vanishes
   silently.** `Path.unite([sq(1e6,1e6,1), sq(1e6+0.5,1e6+0.5,1)])` returns
   `[]` because the tolerance is 1 at that offset. Documented, but a ring that
   had three or more distinct input points and lost them all deserves a
   `Util.warn`, as non-finite input already gets.

9. **`form.bezier` is silent on short input while `line` and `polygon`
   warn.** Use `Util.arrayCheck(p, 4)` in the instance method for
   consistency. The `SVGForm` statics also lack the legacy `DOMFormContext`
   overloads that `line`, `polygon` and `rect` have, so `SVGForm.bezier(domCtx,
pts)` and `SVGForm.compound(domCtx, rings)` would call a missing
   `beginPath`. Add the overloads or state that the legacy DOM statics are
   frozen at the 1.0.0 set.

10. **Demos.** `demo/curve.cardinal.js` describes "a centripetal one converted
    to bezier" but draws only the handles and anchor dots, never the curve;
    either draw it with `form.bezier(bezier)` or reword. `demo/curve.bspline.js`
    computes `anchors` with `bezierToCardinal` and never uses it.

11. **SKILL.md ecosystem paragraph is stale.** It says react-pts-canvas pins
    0.12.8 and pts-cli is unpublished; the Ecosystem guide now names
    `pts-render` on npm and react.ptsjs.org. `guide/md/_0400_Op.md` shows two
    of the four conversions; add `bsplineToBezier` and `bezierToCardinal`
    lines. `guide/md/_9100_Changelog.md` stops at 1.0 and could take a short
    1.0.1 section. `PoissonDisk.setup` lacks `@returns` and `@example`.

## Later, not for 1.0.1

- **Path ray-tree build dominates unions with many holes** (`indexRays`):
  10 000 overlapping 12-gons forming one blob with 9801 holes take 658 ms
  for `unite` versus 239 ms for `divide`, with about 265 ms in the per-level
  closure sorts and slice copies. Sort edge indices once per axis into typed
  arrays and split by rank, and keep node bounds in typed arrays.
- **Path snap-rounding at the rounding scale** (plausible, not triggered by
  fuzzing): phase-2 crossings within the tolerance of an existing vertex
  become new vertices and sub-edges are ordered by projection, so three edges
  through a point at 1e-13 spacing could order inconsistently. Worth a
  targeted test before relying on it.
- **`Bound` with reversed corners** keeps the given top-left and the absolute
  size, so `new Bound([10,10],[0,0])` spans 10..20. `Create.sampling` and
  `distributeRandom` both inherit this. Fix in `Bound` or document; not a
  sampling bug.
- **`PoissonDisk.step` recomputes per-call constants** (the candidate
  rotation, the bound size, the thin-strip flags). About 1 µs per point on a
  2000×2000 bound versus 0.28 µs for `distributeRandom`, which is reasonable
  for Bridson; hoisting into `setup` trims a few percent.
- **API notes.** `Create.sampling` is the only gerund among `distributeRandom`,
  `gridPts`, `radialPts`, `noisePts`, `flock`; `PoissonDisk.step()` returns a
  Pt while `Flock.step()` returns `this`. `PoissonDiskOptions.start` says
  "inside the bound" but the bound is half-open, so a start on the far edge
  throws; say so. A species-derived `PoissonDisk` (from `map`, `slice`)
  reports radius 0 and `done`; one sentence in the class doc. `_radius` and
  `_candidates` are `protected` while the rest are `private`.
- **Bench tooling.** `bench.mjs --against <ref>` should skip cases whose
  function is missing in the reference build instead of aborting the run.

## Test gaps worth closing with the fixes

- Path: a bounded-resource test for many shapes overlapping one region and
  for a fan sharing a vertex (100 spokes with a wall-time or heap sanity
  check); pinched output; the single-Group misuse; a fully collapsed ring;
  feedback of a `divide` result into `unite`; circles with hundreds of
  vertices in the fuzz (it uses 3 to 8 point rings and at most 4 shapes);
  `crossingParameter` at an ordinary value such as 0.25.
- Curves: non-finite and overflowing alpha; parity at tension 0 and above 1
  (it passes but is unpinned); SVG `bezier` with a trailing partial segment
  and a filled chain.
- Sampling: a plain `{x, y, width, height}` bound (works); `setup` on a
  species-derived instance (works); the far-edge `start` rejection as
  intended behavior.

## Verified as correct

Path geometry held up under two independent fuzzes (the shipped one on six
seeds at 4000 trials, plus 3000 area-identity trials with circles, integer
grids, coincident vertices and result feedback) and every degenerate input
probed: empty, under three points, zero area, duplicates, collinear, identical
shapes, shared edges and vertices, point touches, figure-8 and pentagram,
three-deep nesting, an island in a hole, mixed orientation, NaN. Curve
conversions match the samplers within float32 at tensions 0, 0.5, 1, 2 and
−0.5, round trips are exact (cardinal) or within 1.2e-4 (B-spline), and 3D,
plain arrays, generators and partial segments behave. The sampler's
minimum-distance invariant holds on float32 output at offsets up to 1e7, and
seeding, progressive versus one-shot, `step` after `done`, `setup` reuse and
Array species all behave. Every new public method has params, returns and an
example; every `#link` and demo link resolves; the generated docs include all
new classes, methods and types.

## Resolution (2026-09-19)

Everything above except the snap-rounding probe was fixed the same day:

- Path labeling walks the dual graph depth-first with one mutable winding
  vector (three numbers per face); the sweep runs its phases inline and
  buffers the first phase's pairs up to a cap; pinched walks are split into
  simple rings, with a hole's outer found among the pieces of its walk or by
  a ray from the walk's leftmost vertex; the ray tree partitions by median on
  precomputed centers; a single ring is accepted as one shape; a ring that
  collapses under the tolerance warns.
- The alpha guard covers infinity and overflow; `form.bezier` warns on short
  input; `form.compound` warns on a polygon list; `SVGForm` gains the DOM
  overloads; `Bound` orders its corners; the sampler's per-setup constants
  are hoisted; the docs, changelog, guides, demos and editor list are
  updated; the fuzz covers large discs, shifted copies and result feedback;
  the bench A/B skips cases the reference build lacks.
- Measured after the fix: uniting 300 bars through one point takes 0.4 s and
  155 MB (was 1.6 s and 1.27 GB); the 6000-triangle fan completes in about
  2 s; the 512-gon union is a third faster; the small three-shape cases are
  within noise of before.
- Left as documented behavior: the chordal parameterization's tangent
  direction at near-coincident anchors, and the snap-rounding case, which no
  fuzz has triggered.
